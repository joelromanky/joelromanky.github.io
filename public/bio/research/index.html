Research Summary

PostDoc Research

My postdoctoral research at University of Luxembourg - SnT focuses on the trustworthiness of artificial intelligence systems, with particular emphasis on explainability, robustness, and generalization capabilities of machine learning algorithms.

PhD Research

My PhD research at Orange Innovation focused on developing artificial intelligence techniques for anomaly detection on time series and root cause diagnosis of low-latency applications used over time-varying capacities network (4G/5G, WiFi).

Research interests

  • Explainable AI (XAI) for VLMs
  • Trustworthy AI
  • Machine Learning Robustness
  • Anomaly Detection for Time Series
  • Tabular Data
  • Low-Latency Applications
