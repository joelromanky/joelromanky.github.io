## PostDoc Research
---

My postdoctoral research at [University of Luxembourg - SnT](https://www.uni.lu/snt-en/research-groups/serval/) focuses on the trustworthiness of artificial intelligence systems, with particular emphasis on explainability, robustness, and generalization capabilities of machine learning algorithms.

## PhD Research
---

My PhD research at [Orange Innovation](https://www.orange.com/fr) focused on developing artificial intelligence techniques for anomaly detection on time series and root cause diagnosis of low-latency applications used over time-varying capacities network (4G/5G, WiFi).

## Research interests
---

- Explainable AI (XAI) for VLMs
- Trustworthy AI  
- Machine Learning Robustness  
- Anomaly Detection for Time Series 
- Tabular Data 
- Low-Latency Applications
